About

Version — · storage…

Your data

  • Encrypted vault (AES-GCM, PIN-derived key, in localStorage): tasks, routines, saved stories & papers, day-close log with mood.
  • Plain localStorage: preferences, read/unread story ids (capped at 500), reading-streak days, meditation & focus logs, feed timestamp.
  • sessionStorage: the day-close draft — wiped as soon as you close the day.
  • Nothing leaves this device except public content fetches: the news/arXiv feed, weather, and images.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking — clearing site data wipes everything.
